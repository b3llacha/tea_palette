BEHIND THE PALETTE

Hi, I’m Bella Cha.
A taste for the thoughtful.

Tea Palette is my space for looking closely at the consumer products we use every day. I publish design teardowns and develop redesign prototypes that connect a clear critique to a tangible direction.

From restaurant discovery to streaming, learning, and a favorite treat, I’m interested in the small decisions that make a product easier—and more enjoyable—to use.
